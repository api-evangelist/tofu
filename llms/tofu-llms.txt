# Tofu

> Tofu is the Agentic Demand Gen Platform for B2B teams who need to ship more pipeline without adding headcount. Three agents, Research, Create, and Launch: work together to run always-on campaigns across email, LinkedIn, and landing pages, scaling 1:1 ABM to hundreds of accounts inside the martech stack you already use. Tofu learns your brand, messaging, personas, and target accounts, then executes and optimizes campaigns 8× faster than traditional workflows. Trusted by leading mid-market and enterprise B2B companies. SOC 2 certified.
