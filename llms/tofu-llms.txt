# Tofu

> Tofu (tofuhq.com) is an agentic go-to-market (GTM) platform that automates always-on, integrated marketing and sales campaigns inside a team's existing GTM stack — helping teams ship integrated campaigns dramatically faster across outbound prospecting, lead nurturing, event follow-up, sales acceleration, deal re-engagement, and customer expansion. Backed by Index Ventures. No public developer API is documented as of this profile.
